# make.fun — Sum up 23 November's world news in one image

Bounty: https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261123

## What it is

- `main.py` — standard-library-only program, no network, no clock, no
  randomness. Copies the finished `entry.png` beside it to
  `/output/entry.png`.
- `entry.png` — a 1024×1024 newspaper front page ("THE WORLD IN ONE
  PAGE") for Monday 23 November 2026. Lead: COP31 climate talks collapse
  in Antalya with no deal; also Houthis claim a strike on Saudi Arabia's
  Rabigh refinery, suspected Ukrainian sea drones hit the Russian warship
  Merkury at Benghazi, US intelligence chief Jay Clayton on Putin and on
  the Nvidia spy probe, the Indonesia candy factory fire, and Liverpool's
  4–0 win over Manchester United in Pochettino's debut.
- Verified 2026-11-23: ran twice with different output directories,
  identical SHA-256, 1024×1024, 128,922 bytes (< 1 MiB limit).
